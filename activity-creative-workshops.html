Creative Workshops

Drawing, writing and hands-on exercises that turn a legal document into something young people can see themselves in.

Chapter 02 — Strategies & Activities

About this activity

Drawing, writing and hands-on exercises help participants connect abstract constitutional rights to their own lives. Every session includes a creative activity — tracing a hand, illustrating a right, or writing a short reflection.

The results double as teaching tools: facilitators use each participant's own drawing or sentence to open a conversation about how that right is protected under the Constitution of Zimbabwe.
