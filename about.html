Chapters 1–4 · The Constitution of JCC

Founded on values, built for youth.

Everything below is drawn directly from our governing constitution — the same document that binds our founders, advisors and every member who joins us.

Section 01 — Description

An apolitical, youth-led movement

Junior Court Counsel is an apolitical, youth-found and youth-led organisation with the mandate to raise legal awareness amongst young people and adolescents in Zimbabwe — thereby promoting legal empowerment and emancipation, and fostering interest in governance and leadership. We also seek to provide for basic children's rights in marginalised communities, giving them legal awareness alongside practical support.

Section 02 — Supremacy of the Constitution

Our founding document is our highest law

This constitution is the supreme law of the organisation — any code or practice inconsistent with it is void. It binds every member at every level, including founding members, partners and advisors, without exception.

Chapter 02 — Organisation Profile

Vision, motto & objectives

04 — VISION

Legal empowerment for a generation

To create and increase awareness of the legal frameworks governing Zimbabwe amongst youth and adolescents, thereby creating legal empowerment and emancipation.

05 — MOTTO

Know Your Rights.

A short, deliberate phrase — the standard every activity, publication and outreach is measured against.

07 — SUB-OBJECTIVES

Peace, justice, and future experts

To create peace amongst Zimbabwean youth, build just societies for young people to live in, and nurture the next generation of legal experts.

Chapter 03 — Organisation Structure

ORGAN 01

Advisory Board

Exists purely to advise the governing board. All advisors are non-JCC members, the board holds an odd number of seats, at least three advisors at any time, and appointments go strictly on merit and track record.

ORGAN 02

Executive Board

The planning organ responsible for policy formulation. Composed of JCC members in an odd number with gender balance respected at all times: the Founding CEO, Deputy CEO, Secretary and Treasurer.

ORGAN 03

Standing Committee

The implementing agent for all programs and activities, managing human and non-human resources across five departments — open only to JCC members.

Chapter 05 — Meetings

Annual General & ordinary meetings

Our Annual General Meeting is held once a year, toward the end of the financial year, where the Executive Board reports on achievements, members vote on constitutional changes, and policy decisions are made. Ordinary meetings bring the Executive Board, Standing Committee and general membership together once every two months.

Chapter 04 — Finance

Accountable by design

Our financial year ends every November. The organisation's bank account requires two signatures for any commitment — the Founding CEO plus either the Treasurer or Secretary — and an independent accounting officer reviews our records within six months of year-end.
