Community Outreach

Legal awareness doesn't stay on paper — it happens on benches under trees, in open fields, and on doorsteps across Chitungwiza.

Chapter 02 — Strategies & Activities

About this activity

JCC takes legal awareness directly into communities — sitting down on benches under trees, in open fields, and outside people's homes to talk through what the Constitution means for everyday life.

These sessions bring together the Executive Board, Standing Committee, and general membership, and often include whole families — not just the young people the sessions are aimed at.
